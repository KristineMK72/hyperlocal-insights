Zoning Guidance

Zoning is highly local and still incompletely digitized nationwide. The best public resource is the National Zoning Atlas, which has standardized maps for thousands of U.S. jurisdictions.

Open National Zoning Atlas

On the Atlas map, search or pan to this location to see district boundaries, allowed uses, and residential/commercial rules. Coverage is expanding; rural/township data may still be limited.

Practical next steps for a small business

  • Confirm the parcel’s zoning district with the local planning department.
  • Check permitted uses, parking, signage, and any special permits.
  • Ask about recent variances or rezoning activity nearby.
  • Pair this with competitor density and demographics before committing to a lease.
